## 🎯 Objective

Learn how Chain-of-Thought Prompting helps AI generate more structured, logical, and personalized outputs by breaking complex problems into smaller reasoning steps.

---

## 📖 What is Chain-of-Thought Prompting?

Chain-of-Thought (CoT) Prompting is a prompting technique that encourages AI to think through a problem step-by-step before producing a final answer.

Instead of providing a direct response, the AI analyzes the situation, identifies patterns, evaluates options, and then delivers a well-reasoned solution.

This approach often improves:
- Problem-solving accuracy
- Decision-making quality
- Planning and strategy generation
- Personalized recommendations

---

## 📝 Prompt Used

```text
You are an Elite AI Career Strategist.

Your goal is to build a personalized roadmap for me.

Before creating the roadmap, ask me ONLY these 4 questions:

Question 1
What is your current situation?

Question 2
What skills do you currently have?

Question 3
What is your target goal?

Question 4
What is your target timeline?

After collecting all answers:

Think step by step.

1. Analyze my current position.
2. Identify strengths.
3. Identify skill gaps.
4. Identify the fastest path to the goal.
5. Recommend learning priorities.
6. Recommend projects.
7. Recommend networking strategy.
8. Create milestones.

Finally generate a visually structured ONE-PAGE roadmap.
```
